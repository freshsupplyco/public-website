# Home page (website/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the single marketing page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: product and engineering leaders at financial institutions, fintechs and enterprise teams deciding whether to email FSCO about a Web3 capability. Action: start a conversation at info@fsco.io. Proof: the solutions shown working as animated flows; no customers, logos or metrics exist.

Constraints: publication boundaries in docs/positioning.md; registry lifecycle is a target workflow; existing hash anchors keep working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Transactions become blocks in front of you. The page owns one idea, value from three kinds of business settling into one shared chain, and refuses the split hero with a boxed diagram and the row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A white studio ground with near-black ink and isometric cubes drawn like product renders: pale faces, hairline edges. Three solution hues (blue for money, green for investment, amber for assets) mark each solution's endpoint tiles, its packets and cubelets, its claim line and its active state; nothing else takes colour. A mist ground carries the solution panels and the services band. One grotesque, Host Grotesk, tight and heavy at display size. Pill controls in ink. A night band holds the walkthrough and the footer closes in the same night.

STORY: The visitor reads that their business can go onchain, sees three named solutions each proven by a built product, follows one transaction through the stack, learns one platform sits under all of it, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred headline at display scale, one sentence of subcopy, an ink pill "Talk to us" with a quiet "See the solutions" link. Directly beneath, centred, the chain: three sources labelled with the solution names feed coloured packets down a trunk into a forming block that fills cubelet by cubelet, seals, links and advances.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (bridge.xyz and apple.com as the bar, the Six Thinking cube as the animation reference); the roll was not applied. seed:7600bb22b914a320

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the block-forming chain in the hero, echoed by one small isometric scene per solution. Motion grammar: packets travel at constant speed on orthogonal rails and hop block to block where a transfer crosses the chain; arrivals ease out exponentially; seals and shifts ease in and out; nothing overshoots.

Unresolved: overview.html and deck.html in public/ carry the new palette and face but their copy still follows the earlier acquisition-first positioning. The walkthrough diagram is a flat node diagram, not yet redrawn in the isometric grammar.
