Three products. A shared foundation.

Sendr is a white-label remittance experience connecting customer onboarding and transfer initiation with stablecoin settlement and local payout workflows.

JUST Finance connects financial offerings to Web3 wallets, with interfaces for balances, provider-based savings, deposits, withdrawals and transaction activity.

Environmental Registry is a product foundation for environmental markets, connecting project records and human review with a planned digital credit lifecycle. Issuance, transfer and retirement integrations are scoped for each registry implementation.

Operational visibility: Mission Control provides a view of platform events, wallets, transactions and contract activity.
